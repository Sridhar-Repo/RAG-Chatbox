# Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to three days a week with manager approval.

Core hours are 11:00 to 15:00 local time, when everyone should be reachable.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop and a monitor. A one-time stipend of $400 covers a desk or chair.
Internet costs are reimbursed up to $40 per month with a receipt.

## Security

Use the company VPN on public networks. Never store customer data on personal devices.
